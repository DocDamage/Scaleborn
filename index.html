CHAPTER ZERO · THE FIRST FLIGHT

The Azure Coast awaits.

Your dragon knows the winds. Explore the golden cliffs, fishing harbor and distant island. This is an early, playable development build—not the complete adventure. Land before shrinking; Tab switches between the rider and small dragon. Over deep water, F splashes down and C dives. The rider and dragon share magical underwater breathing. Find the Gloam hounds east of Tidegate for an early battle; R or primary mouse click breathes fire.
